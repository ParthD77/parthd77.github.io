P-Bot is a personal Telegram assistant that prepares concise briefings from authorized Gmail accounts and selected news sources. Access is restricted to approved users.

Read the privacy policy View terms of service
Personal-use application
  • Uses Google OAuth to connect only accounts explicitly authorized by their owner.
  • Requests read-only Gmail access and does not send, delete, archive, or modify email.
  • Delivers summaries through a private Telegram bot restricted to approved user IDs.

How it works

A focused briefing pipeline

The application collects only the information needed to create a requested briefing, processes it for that purpose, and presents the result to an authorized user.

1. Authorize

The account owner signs in through Google's OAuth flow and grants the displayed permissions.

2. Summarize

P-Bot reads relevant message data and generates a concise personal briefing.

3. Deliver

The briefing is sent only through the private Telegram interface configured for the application.
