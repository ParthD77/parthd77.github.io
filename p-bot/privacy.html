Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 24, 2026

Overview

P-Bot is a private personal-use application operated by Parth Dhroovji. It creates briefings from information supplied by authorized services, including Gmail and selected news sources, and delivers those briefings through Telegram.

This policy explains what information P-Bot accesses, why it is used, how it is protected, and how authorization can be withdrawn.

Information P-Bot accesses

When a user connects a Google account, P-Bot may access Gmail data permitted by the OAuth scopes shown during authorization. The application is designed to use read-only Gmail access.

  • Email metadata, including sender, subject, date, labels, and message identifiers.
  • Email content needed to identify relevant messages and generate a briefing.
  • Google OAuth credentials, including access and refresh tokens, needed to maintain the authorized connection.
  • Telegram identifiers and messages required to authenticate an approved user and respond to commands.
  • Basic operational logs, configuration data, and user feedback needed to maintain and improve the application.
P-Bot does not request permission to send email, delete email, archive email, apply labels, or mark messages as read.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide and operate P-Bot. This includes authenticating approved users, retrieving relevant messages, creating requested briefings, delivering results, troubleshooting failures, and protecting the application from unauthorized access.

P-Bot does not sell personal information, use Gmail data for advertising, or provide Gmail data to data brokers.

Google user data

P-Bot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is not used to build advertising profiles, serve targeted advertisements, determine creditworthiness, or train general-purpose artificial intelligence models.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and application data are stored only in systems controlled by the operator. Reasonable technical safeguards are used, including access restrictions, secret management, encryption where configured, and limiting the application to approved Telegram user IDs.

No internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure. Access should be revoked immediately if credentials or the hosting environment are suspected to be compromised.

Data retention

Data is retained only for as long as needed to operate the application, troubleshoot problems, maintain authorized connections, or satisfy legal obligations. Generated briefings and logs may be deleted periodically. OAuth credentials are retained until the connected account is removed, authorization is revoked, or the application data is deleted.

Sharing and service providers

P-Bot may transmit information to infrastructure and service providers strictly as needed to operate the application, such as Google, Telegram, hosting providers, databases, and configured artificial intelligence or news services. These services process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to investigate abuse, or to protect the security and rights of the operator or users.

Revoking access and deleting data

A user can revoke P-Bot's Google access from the security settings of their Google Account. The operator can also remove the connected account and delete its stored OAuth credentials and associated application data.

Because P-Bot is currently a private application, deletion or privacy requests can be submitted directly to the operator through the contact method listed on the main portfolio website at parthdhroovji.me.

Children's privacy

P-Bot is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age required to consent to data processing in their jurisdiction.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application, its integrations, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.
